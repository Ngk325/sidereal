# Sidereal Ink

**A generative movement for figures drawn out of a single moment.**

Every moment has a geometry. Not a mystical one necessarily — a literal one: an angle of the earth, a latitude, a set of integers that can only be assembled that way once. *Sidereal Ink* takes such a set and refuses to illustrate it. Instead it lets the numbers become **constraints on a physical system**, and then lets that system run until it settles. The figure that appears was never drawn; it was permitted.

The armature is arithmetic. A digit sum decides how many points sit on the governing circle; a reduced root decides the rotational order of the whole field; the hour and minute become a single angle that tilts every layer off true, so nothing in the composition is ever quite square with the frame. This slight, deliberate obliquity is the movement's signature — the mark of a **meticulously crafted algorithm** where the offset was tuned degree by degree until the imbalance read as intention rather than error.

Over the armature runs ink. Thousands of particles are released from the vertices of the governing polygon into a layered noise field, and each particle's path is mirrored around the center at the field's rotational order. No particle knows it is part of a mandala. Symmetry is not painted — it is a **consequence of replication under a shared law**, which is why the result breathes where a mirrored image would merely repeat. Density accumulates where trajectories agree; voids open where the field disagrees with itself. These voids are the most valuable thing the algorithm produces and must never be filled.

Chord webs bind the whole. Points on the outer circle connect by modular multiplication — every point to its multiple — and the resulting cardioid and nephroid envelopes emerge as pure number, no curve ever specified. This is the **product of deep computational expertise**: the understanding that the most beautiful curves in the piece should be ones nobody wrote, only implied by an integer step.

Color is austere and stratified. A near-black ground stands for the hour before dawn. Gold carries structure; ember carries motion; a bone-pale wash carries the residue of both. Nothing is saturated for its own sake. The palette was arrived at by **painstaking optimization** — the restraint is the craft.

The work is finished when accumulation stops changing the read of the figure. Each seed reveals a different facet of the same lawful moment: the arithmetic never varies, only the ink's dispersion. That is the whole claim of *Sidereal Ink* — a fixed moment, infinite renderings, and a **master-level implementation** in which every parameter earns its place.
